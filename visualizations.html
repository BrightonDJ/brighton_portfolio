Interactive Visualizations

Hands-on explanations of the math.

Small interactive demos for the concepts covered on the Research page — built to give intuition, not to reproduce any client data.

Bounding Box Rotation
Drag the slider to see how a rotated bounding box (x, y, w, h, θ) tracks an object's true orientation, versus a fixed axis-aligned box.

Attention Visualization
Hover a cell to see a simplified self-attention pattern — how strongly that position "attends" to every other position in the grid.
Hover any cell — brighter cells indicate higher attention weight from the hovered position.

Hungarian Matching
Click "Match" to see predictions assigned to ground-truth boxes via lowest-cost bipartite matching — the set-prediction training approach used in Oriented-DETR.
Each prediction is assigned to exactly one ground-truth box, minimizing total assignment cost.
